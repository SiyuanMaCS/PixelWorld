Method

Generation

generate.py loads first_frame.png and copies it exactly as frame 00. For frames 01 through 19, it starts from a numerically inpainted version of the first frame where the initially held ball/block regions are filled from nearby RGB samples. It then redraws:

  • two open grabber tools using simple line/ellipse primitives in sampled dark metal tones,
  • a brown tennis ball with procedural circular shading, small felt texture, seam arcs, and a soft contact shadow,
  • an orange block with a rotated quadrilateral body, top/side faces, edge highlights, and a contact shadow.

Object motion is analytic keyframed at 4 fps: both objects release near the measured initial clusters, accelerate downward, contact the pillows/table area, bounce or rock briefly, and settle by frame 19. The camera remains locked off; no global image motion is applied.

Output and verification

  • frame 00 exactly matches first_frame.png,
  • pixels.npz has dtype uint32 and shape (144, 256, 20),
